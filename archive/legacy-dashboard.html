Hunger strike tips
Ball pythons are notorious hunger strikers — especially juveniles and during seasonal changes. If Casper refuses:

1. Check temperatures — cool side too cold is the #1 cause
2. Check humidity — below 60% causes stress
3. Try a different prey size or scent (wash hands before handling prey)
4. Try "braining" — pierce the skull to release scent
5. Cover the enclosure during feeding for privacy
6. A healthy adult BP can safely fast for 6+ months — don't panic

Lily White morph diet note
The Lily White morph is a co-dominant gene that produces the distinctive white/cream dorsal coloration. There are no special dietary requirements for this morph — care is identical to standard crested geckos. The brownish areas Erica sees are the base coat typical of Lily Whites, which varies in expression.
